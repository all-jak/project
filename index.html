Europe Work Visa Guide 2026

Which European countries give work visas to Bangladeshis, what each one asks for, where you submit the visa, and where to find a job offer first.

Job offer first

Almost every country needs an employer to apply for your permit before you apply for a visa.

Easiest without a degree

Romania and Croatia, then Italy, Greece and Malta.

Best for developers

Germany's IT route, the Netherlands, Sweden, Ireland, Cyprus's tech fast-track, and Spain's remote-work visa.

Avoid for now

Hungary (closed), Serbia and North Macedonia (BMET clearance on hold), Russia, Belarus, and Northern Cyprus (not the EU).

Outside Europe

New Zealand: a developer job there can lead to permanent residence after 2 years of work, with no degree. Mauritius: a developer job paying MUR 50,000 a month, or a free visa for remote work.

Your plan

A plan built for your profile

EDU SSC (no HSC) EDU GED is optional SKILL Web & app developer AGE 29 LANG Good English ALSO Farm and supervisor work

Your developer experience matters more than your certificates. Several countries give IT workers a visa without a degree if you can prove about three years of work and the job pays enough. Run Plan A and Plan B together, and keep Plan C ready as a backup.

Do this first

Collect proof of your experience. Germany's IT route and Spain's remote-work visa both accept about 3 years of documented work instead of a degree. Get experience letters on company letterhead (dates, job title, tech stack), contracts, payslips or bank statements, tax returns, freelance invoices and platform history, plus links to your live apps and GitHub.

A

Tech job with visa sponsorship

Best fit
Why it fits you
No degree is needed for Germany's IT route, the Netherlands, Sweden or Ireland, as long as the salary and your experience are right.
Targets
  • Germany: EU Blue Card for IT specialists, €45,934 a year, 3 years' experience in the last 7.
  • Netherlands: €4,357 a month under 30, recognised-sponsor employer. You are 29: the IND must receive your application before your 30th birthday, or the €5,942 rate applies.
  • Sweden: SEK 34,470 a month, no education rule.
  • Ireland: €36,605 a year, experience can replace a degree, English-speaking.
  • Poland: big IT sector; the employer gets your work permit.
  • Cyprus: foreign-owned tech companies, mostly in Limassol, need €2,500 a month and a degree or 2 years' experience. See Cyprus.
Steps
  1. Collect proof of 3+ years' experience (see above).
  2. Take IELTS and aim for 6.5 or higher to prove your English.
  3. Polish your GitHub and 2–3 live projects; write a one-page English CV.
  4. Apply to 10+ jobs a week on the sponsorship boards; contact companies on sponsor lists.
  5. After an offer, the employer handles the permit. Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden take your visa in Dhaka.

B

Remote job, then a digital-nomad visa

Run in parallel
Why it fits you
You can work for foreign companies or clients from Dhaka now, then move. Spain accepts 3 years' experience instead of a degree.
Targets
  • Spain: €2,849 a month of remote income; the residence permit can be renewed.
  • Portugal: €3,680 a month plus €11,040 in savings.
  • Greece: €3,500 a month net. Malta: €42,000 a year. Cyprus: €3,500 a month net.
  • Mauritius (outside Europe): a free Premium Visa with US$1,500 a month of foreign income. See Mauritius.
  • Croatia: €3,622.50 a month, but only up to 18 months.
Steps
  1. Get a steady remote job or long-term clients outside the country you want to move to.
  2. Take your pay through your bank so every month is on record; keep contracts.
  3. Build at least 3–6 months of steady income before you apply (Portugal checks the last 3 months).
  4. Apply for Spain at the Spanish Embassy in Dhaka (BLS); Portugal through its embassy in New Delhi.

C

Farm or supervisor work

Backup
Why it fits you
Your SSC is enough and no degree is needed. Pay is low and the permit ties you to one employer, so treat it as a backup.
Targets
  • Greece: BOESL's government seasonal farm programme (4,000 a year).
  • Italy: seasonal farm quota; the farm files for you on the 12 January 2027 click day.
  • Romania: farm and construction jobs on the 289-job shortage list.
  • Croatia: seasonal tourism and farm permits for up to 3 years.
  • Montenegro: summer hotel and building jobs, visa in Dhaka. Watch for a government deal (details).
Steps
  1. Register on BMET's Overseas Employment Platform and follow BOESL circulars.
  2. Use only licensed agencies; check the RL number before paying anything.
  3. For supervisor jobs, get letters proving you supervised people.
  4. For Italy, the employer must prepare your file before the January 2027 click day.

Also worth a look

New Zealand. A developer job there can lead to permanent residence after 2 years of work, with no degree and no English test for the work visa. Jobs are scarce right now, so run it next to Plan A. See the New Zealand section.

Do you need the GED?

Not for these visas. Work visas for developers look at your experience and the salary, not school certificates. A GED also does not open Germany's Opportunity Card or German public universities. Take it only if an employer asks for high-school equivalency, or if you later want to study abroad.

      Official costs · checked 18 October 2026

      Doing it yourself: the real cost with a job offer

      With a real job offer you can do every step yourself and pay only government fees, the visa centre's charge, your own documents and your ticket. No agent and no "processing fee". Here is that cost for every country in this guide, in the currency each fee is charged in.

      Who pays what

      In most countries the employer applies and pays for its own permit or approval. You pay the visa fee, the visa centre's service charge, your residence card after arrival, and your costs in Bangladesh. In Romania, Malta, Ireland and New Zealand the law also stops employers or licensed agencies from passing these costs on to you.

      Do it yourself in 7 steps

      1. Find the job yourself on the portals in this guide, and get a signed offer or contract.
      2. Check the employer exists and, where there is a list, is allowed to hire foreigners.
      3. The employer applies for your permit and pays its own fee.
      4. Book your own appointment on the embassy or visa-centre website. Appointments are free; never buy one.
      5. Pay the visa fee yourself at the counter or online, and keep every receipt.
      6. Get BMET clearance on oc.bmet.gov.bd and do the pre-departure training.
      7. Buy your own ticket, unless the employer pays for it.

              Where you apply · checked 21 October 2026

              Dhaka or India: where you hand in your work visa

              After your employer's permit is approved, you apply for the visa. For 20 countries you can do that from Bangladesh: at an embassy or visa centre in Dhaka, online, or by post. For 13 you must travel to India, almost always to New Delhi.

              If you must go to India

              India gives Bangladeshis who need to visit a foreign embassy in India a double-entry "X" visa, from the Indian High Commission in Dhaka. It is valid for up to 3 months and allows up to 15 days in India. Portugal and Lithuania say they will not accept you without it. The cheapest trip costs about Tk 17,700 plus a hotel; see Official costs.

                              Country deep dive · checked 20 October 2026

                              Cyprus: the EU's Greek-Cypriot south, not the north

                              The Republic of Cyprus is an EU country short of workers on farms, in factories, shops, delivery and building, and its foreign-owned tech companies can hire developers without a degree. The employer applies for you. Avoid anyone who offers "Cyprus" through the Turkish-Cypriot north.

                              Greek Cyprus or Turkish Cyprus?

                              The Cyprus in this guide is the Republic of Cyprus: the Greek-Cypriot south, an EU member. Northern Cyprus is run by Turkish Cypriots, is recognised only by Turkey and is not in the EU. Agents sell its student visas as a back door to Europe. Cyprus says about 9 in 10 irregular migrants arrive through Turkey and the north and then cross the UN buffer zone. Most Bangladeshis who do this are refused asylum.

                              What changed in 2025–2026, and what's next

                                1

                                Developer job at a foreign-owned company

                                Best fit
                                Why it fits you
                                Foreign-owned companies, many of them in tech around Limassol, can hire non-EU staff on a fast-track that accepts 2 years of relevant experience instead of a degree. There is no labour-market test and no cap on these staff.
                                You need
                                • A job paying at least €2,500 a month before tax, on a contract of at least 2 years.
                                • A degree, or proof of 2 years' relevant experience: letters, contracts and your portfolio.
                                • An employer registered with the Business Facilitation Unit.
                                Good to know
                                • Permits take about a month and last up to 3 years, renewable.
                                • The EU Blue Card (€43,632 a year) also accepts 3 years' IT experience instead of a degree.
                                2

                                Farm, factory, shop, delivery or building job

                                Employer route
                                Jobs open to you
                                Agriculture, livestock, food delivery, manufacturing, wholesale and retail, construction and other short-staffed jobs.
                                How it works
                                • The employer first offers the job to local and EU workers through the public employment service.
                                • The Department of Labour approves the vacancy (about 3 months), then the Migration Department issues your entry permit.
                                • Each company may hire only a limited share of non-EU staff.
                                Pay and limits
                                At least €979 a month for your first 6 months with an employer, then €1,088. A general-employment permit lasts at most 4 years in total (6 in farming and livestock).
                                3

                                Digital nomad visa

                                Plan B
                                What it is
                                Live in Cyprus while you work for employers or clients outside Cyprus: 1 year at first, up to 3 years in total.
                                You need
                                At least €3,500 a month after tax, health insurance and a clean police record. There are 1,000 places a year since 30 October 2025.

                                From job offer to residence permit, step by step

                                1. Find an employerErgodotisi, Carierista or CyprusWork; for the fast-track, foreign-owned tech companies.
                                2. Local workers firstGeneral jobs only: the employer advertises through the public employment service before it may hire you.
                                3. ApprovalDepartment of Labour, about 3 months. Fast-track companies skip this step.
                                4. Entry permitThe employer applies to the Migration Department with your contract.
                                5. Visa at VFS DhakaThe Cyprus High Commission in New Delhi decides. Check with VFS before you book any trip to Delhi.
                                6. BMET clearance, then flyTk 6,500; Dhaka → Larnaca about US$470–652 one way.
                                7. Register within 7 daysAt the Migration Department: residence permit €70 + Aliens' Register €70.

                                    Country deep dive · checked 21 October 2026

                                    Malta: English-speaking jobs, stricter checks

                                    Malta is a small EU island where work runs in English. Hotels, cleaning and building firms hire through an employer-led single permit, and iGaming and tech companies hire developers. Rules have tightened since 2024: a compulsory course, stricter checks on employers, and no new permits for food couriers or cab drivers. You apply for the visa at VFS in Dhaka.

                                    Before you pay anyone

                                    A real Maltese job starts with the employer advertising it in Malta and the EU, then applying to Identità for your single permit. You book VFS only after Identità's approval letter arrives. Licensed Maltese agencies may not charge you, and pay below the €994 monthly minimum is illegal. Some Bangladeshi agencies have sold €600-a-month jobs: walk away from those.

                                    What changed in 2024–2026

                                      1

                                      Developer job in iGaming or tech

                                      Best fit
                                      Why it fits you
                                      Malta's iGaming, fintech and software companies work in English and hire foreign developers. Juniors earn about €22,000–30,000 a year; typical engineers €42,000–56,000.
                                      Permit options
                                      • Single permit: any job, after the employer advertises it for 3 weeks.
                                      • Specialist Employee Initiative: €30,000 a year or more, with a degree, or a related qualification and 3 years' experience. Decision in 15 working days.
                                      • Key Employee Initiative: €45,000 a year or more, for senior or highly technical jobs; proof of experience can count.
                                      Good to know
                                      The EU Blue Card (about €38,000 a year) needs a degree. An IT diploma or certificate may count as a related qualification for the fast-track, but your SSC alone probably won't, so ask the employer.
                                      2

                                      Hotel, cleaning or building job

                                      Main route
                                      How it works
                                      • The employer applies to Identità for your single permit: €600, then €150 for each renewal year.
                                      • Hotel and tourism jobs also need the Skills Pass (€230 + €5).
                                      • Everyone must first pay for and pass the €250 pre-departure course.
                                      Pay
                                      At least €994 a month before tax in 2026. Some sectors pay more under their own wage rules.
                                      Closed jobs
                                      No new permits for food couriers or cab drivers since July 2024.
                                      3

                                      Nomad Residence Permit

                                      Plan B
                                      What it is
                                      Live in Malta while you work for employers or clients abroad, renewed one year at a time.
                                      You need
                                      At least €42,000 a year of income from outside Malta.

                                      From job offer to residence card, step by step

                                      1. Find an employerJobsplus, Keepmeposted or jobsinmalta.com; iGaming and tech firms for developer jobs.
                                      2. Local and EU workers firstThe employer advertises on Jobsplus and EURES for 3 weeks (2 weeks for fast-track jobs).
                                      3. Pre-departure course€250, online modules of about 10–12 hours each, then an interview. Hotel jobs also need the Skills Pass.
                                      4. Approval letterThe employer applies to Identità. Up to 4 months, or 15 working days on the fast-track.
                                      5. D visa at VFS DhakaWithin 60 days of the letter. Service charge Tk 13,935–20,900.
                                      6. BMET clearance, then flyTk 6,500; Dhaka → Malta from about US$458 one way.
                                      7. Card in MaltaDo the health screening and give your biometrics, then collect your residence card.

                                          Country deep dive · checked 18 October 2026

                                          Montenegro: talk of 50,000 jobs, but no deal yet

                                          Montenegro is short of summer workers and has started talking to Bangladesh about hiring through a government deal. Nothing is signed. Today, jobs go through a yearly permit quota that Turkish, Serbian and Russian workers mostly fill, and Bangladeshis can now apply for the visa in Dhaka.

                                          What you probably saw

                                          On 5 October 2026, Montenegrin MP Gordan Stojović told Bangladesh's State Minister for Foreign Affairs in Dhaka that his country needs more than 50,000 workers a year, in tourism, construction, hospitality and caregiving, and is open to hiring Bangladeshis through government-to-government agreements. That is an offer to talk, not a quota for Bangladesh: the real 2026 limit is 28,988 permits for all nationalities. If a deal is signed, it would most likely be announced through BOESL and BMET.

                                          What changed in 2025–2026

                                            1

                                            Hotel, restaurant or building job

                                            Main route
                                            Why it fits you
                                            No degree is needed, the summer shortage is real, and your farm and supervisor experience counts for hotel, kitchen and site work.
                                            You need

                                            • The season runs from about May to September, so apply in winter and spring.
                                            • Some ads ask for Montenegrin as well as English.
                                            2

                                            Developer job or remote work

                                            Small market
                                            What changed
                                            Since 17 January 2026, IT and healthcare workers with a contract of 12 months or more can get a combined permit for up to 3 years, renewable once.
                                            The catch
                                            The tech scene is small and pay is low: Paylab's survey puts most programmers at €604–2,180 a month before tax. Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden pay far more for the same work (Plan A).
                                            Remote option
                                            Montenegro has a digital nomad permit for up to 2 years, renewable for 2 more. Guides disagree on the income needed (about €1,350–1,800 a month) and on whether it continues after 2026, so check before you plan around it.

                                                Outside Europe · checked 20 October 2026

                                                Mauritius: a developer route, while factory hiring stays limited

                                                Mauritius, an island country in the Indian Ocean where business runs in English and French, has hired Bangladeshis for decades, mostly for garment factories. Since 2023 it has hired few Bangladeshi factory workers while a government deal is negotiated. For you, the better routes are an IT job paying MUR 50,000 a month, or its free visa for remote workers.

                                                Not a way into Europe

                                                Mauritius is an independent country east of Madagascar. A Mauritian permit does not let you live or work in Europe, and that includes the nearby French island of Réunion. It is in this guide because Bangladeshis have long worked there and its IT sector works in English.

                                                What changed in 2023–2026

                                                  1

                                                  Developer job on an Occupation Permit

                                                  Best fit
                                                  Why it fits you
                                                  Mauritius has a large IT and outsourcing sector, and one Occupation Permit covers both work and residence, for up to 10 years if your contract is that long.
                                                  You need
                                                  • A job with a basic salary of at least MUR 50,000 a month (the 2026 rule for every sector).
                                                  • An employer that applies for you online at the Economic Development Board (EDB).
                                                  • Experience letters and a portfolio. We found no source saying a degree is required, but check with the employer.
                                                  Good to know
                                                  • Mid-level developers earn about MUR 45,000–70,000 a month, so aim for mid-level or senior jobs.
                                                  • You can apply for permanent residence after 5 years.
                                                  2

                                                  Premium Visa: live there, work remotely

                                                  Plan B
                                                  What it is
                                                  A free visa for up to 1 year, renewable, for people whose work and income come from outside Mauritius.
                                                  You need
                                                  About US$1,500 a month of foreign income, and health and travel insurance. You may not take a job with a Mauritian employer.
                                                  Compared with Europe
                                                  The income bar is lower than Spain's (€2,849 a month) or Cyprus's (€3,500), but it does not lead to residence in Europe. Check on the EDB website that your nationality can apply.
                                                  3

                                                  Factory, bakery or printing job

                                                  Limited
                                                  Where it stands
                                                  Since September 2025 Mauritius has hired Bangladeshis only for short-staffed bakery, textile and printing jobs. The minimum wage is MUR 17,745 a month.
                                                  What's coming
                                                  A government deal (MoU) under which Bangladeshi government bodies recruit and check workers before they leave, and a new Technical permit for government-to-government recruits. Neither has started.
                                                  What to do now
                                                  • Register on BMET's Overseas Employment Platform and follow BOESL circulars.
                                                  • Don't pay anyone who sells a place in a "Mauritius G2G" scheme: none exists yet.

                                                  From job offer to residence permit, step by step

                                                  1. Find an employerMyJob.mu, the government's Mauritius Jobs portal or LinkedIn. For factory jobs, wait for BOESL.
                                                  2. The employer appliesOccupation Permit online at the EDB (MUR 50,000+), or a work permit from the labour ministry for other jobs.
                                                  3. Approval, then travelTravel only after the permit is approved; for a work permit you have 6 months to arrive.
                                                  4. Papers and BMET clearancePolice clearance, attested documents and BMET clearance (Tk 6,500).
                                                  5. Fly to MauritiusAbout US$423–835 one way with one stop.
                                                  6. Residence permitAt the Passport and Immigration Office; the employer pays MUR 500 a year.

                                                      Outside Europe · checked 8 October 2026

                                                      New Zealand: a door for skilled workers

                                                      Yes, New Zealand takes skilled workers from abroad, but only through employers it has accredited, and there is no special quota for Bangladeshis. For you, the realistic route is a developer job on a work visa, then permanent residence after 2 years. No degree needed.

                                                      What you probably saw

                                                      Bangladeshi news sites reported New Zealand's changes as "two new visas" and "new rules for skilled workers". The two new visas are seasonal farm and tourism visas, and both ask for earlier seasonal experience. The bigger news for you is the residence pathway that opened on 24 August 2026: it counts work experience instead of a degree.

                                                      What changed in 2025–2026

                                                        1

                                                        Developer job on a work visa (AEWV)

                                                        Best fit
                                                        Why it fits you
                                                        No degree needed. Two years of provable developer work passes the skills test, and developer jobs (skill level 1) need no English test for this visa.
                                                        You need
                                                        • A job offer from an accredited employer: 30+ hours a week, paid the market rate and at least NZ$23.95 an hour.
                                                        • 2+ years of relevant, verifiable experience (or a Level 4+ qualification, or pay of NZ$70+ an hour).
                                                        • A medical at IOM Dhaka and a police certificate.
                                                        Good to know
                                                        • Up to 5 years for skill level 1–3 jobs. The visa is tied to your employer; a new employer needs its own job check.
                                                        • Your partner can get an open work visa if you earn NZ$28.00+ an hour in a skill level 1–3 job.
                                                        • Fee NZ$1,540. About 4 in 5 decisions take 7 weeks or less.
                                                        2

                                                        Residence after 2 years (Skilled Migrant)

                                                        The goal
                                                        Why it fits you
                                                        Since 24 August 2026, experience and New Zealand pay can stand in for a degree.
                                                        Two ways in
                                                        • Skilled Work Experience pathway: 5 years' relevant experience in a skill level 1–3 job, including 2 years in New Zealand at NZ$38.50+ an hour. Amber-list jobs (such as ICT support and web administrator) need all 5 years in New Zealand at NZ$42.00. Red-list jobs can't use it.
                                                        • 6 points: NZ$52.50 an hour (3 points) + 2 years here (3 points), or NZ$70 (4) + 18 months (2), or NZ$105 alone (6).
                                                        Also needed
                                                        Age 55 or under, IELTS 6.5 or equal, health and character checks, and a job paying at least NZ$35.00 an hour. Fee NZ$6,450.
                                                        Shortcut, probably not for you
                                                        Software Engineer is on the Green List's straight-to-residence tier, but it asks for a relevant degree or a long experience record (one guide says 7+ years) and a set salary. Check INZ's Green List for your exact job.
                                                        3

                                                        Farm and seasonal work

                                                        Backup
                                                        Why it is a backup
                                                        Your farm and supervisor skills count, but New Zealand wants proof of earlier seasons, and most farm jobs need an English test.
                                                        Options
                                                        • Global Workforce Seasonal Visa: 3 seasons in a similar job in the last 6 years. Up to 9 months' work a year for 3 years. No English test; the employer invites you.
                                                        • Peak Seasonal Visa: 1 season in the last 3 years. Up to 7 months, then 4 months outside New Zealand. Health insurance for jobs longer than 3 months.
                                                        • Dairy farm assistant on an AEWV: 2 years' farm experience and IELTS 4.0 or equal. Ads pay about NZ$26–30 an hour. The stay is capped at 3 years, and your family can't get visas through this job.
                                                        Good to know
                                                        The seasonal rules don't say your seasons must be in New Zealand, so a farm season in Greece or Italy (Plan C) may count. Confirm with INZ first. Partners and children can't join on either seasonal visa. The Pacific-only RSE scheme and the working holiday visa are not open to Bangladeshis.

                                                        Residence check

                                                        Set the pay you expect in New Zealand. Only full-time work (30+ hours a week) in a skill level 1–3 job paid at least NZ$35.00 an hour counts as New Zealand experience.
