## 1. The short answer

- **Almost every country requires a job offer first.** The employer applies for your permit inside its own country. After that permit is approved, you apply for a long-stay "D" work visa. You cannot get a European work visa on your own without an employer.
- **The one real exception is Germany's Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte).** It is a job-search visa, and Bangladeshis can apply for it in Dhaka. It is only for people with a degree or a 2-year vocational qualification (details in Section B).
- **Non-skilled or semi-skilled workers** (factory, construction, farm, hotel/restaurant, driving, welding, cleaning):
  - Most realistic in 2026: **Romania, Croatia, Italy, Greece, Malta**
  - Next: **Bulgaria, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Cyprus**
  - See [Section A](#section-a-non-skilled-and-semi-skilled-workers).
- **Skilled workers** (degree, diploma, recognised trade certificate, IT, nursing, engineering):
  - Start with **Germany**, then **Finland, Cyprus, Austria, Ireland**.
  - Spain, France, the Netherlands, the Nordic countries and the UK are harder, because they demand high salaries or degree-level jobs.
  - See [Section B](#section-b-skilled-workers).
- **Closed or risky right now:**
  - **Hungary** stopped taking new guest workers on 6 June 2026.
  - The **Czech Republic** has almost no places for Bangladeshis.
  - **BMET clearance for Serbia and North Macedonia** is currently held up (September 2026).
  - In **Russia and Belarus**, workers have been tricked into the army.
  - **Northern Cyprus** is not in the EU; its student visas are sold as a back door to Europe. See [the Cyprus section](#country-deep-dive-cyprus).
  - **Never use the "boat" or "Balkan" routes.** See [section 6](#6-closed-or-risky-right-now).
- **Outside Europe: New Zealand.** A developer job there can lead to permanent residence after 2 years of work, with no degree. Jobs are scarce right now. See [the New Zealand section](#outside-europe-new-zealand).
- **Outside Europe: Mauritius.** A developer job paying MUR 50,000 a month, or a free Premium Visa if you earn US$1,500 a month remotely. Hiring of Bangladeshi factory workers is limited. See [the Mauritius section](#outside-europe-mauritius).
- **Dhaka or India:** 20 countries take your visa application in Bangladesh (Dhaka, online or by post); 13 need a trip to India. See [WHERE YOU APPLY](#where-you-apply-dhaka-or-india).
- **Bangladesh–Europe numbers, first half of 2026:**
  - Legal job migration to Europe rose **46% to 18,220 workers**, while Gulf hiring fell.
  - Italy was the largest European destination (**4,645 workers**).
  - Recruitment is also rising for Portugal, Serbia and Romania.
  - Europe gave only ~5% of Bangladesh's overseas jobs but ~19.6% of its remittances.
  - Source: [TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/world/europe-job-migration-surges-46-h1-amid-gulf-slump-1475796).

## YOUR PLAN: for your profile

**Your profile:** age 29 · SSC (no HSC) · GED is optional · web & app developer · good English · open to farm and supervisor work.

Your developer experience matters more than your certificates. Several countries give IT workers a visa **without a degree** if you can prove about three years of work and the job pays enough. Run **Plan A and Plan B together**, and keep **Plan C** ready as a backup.

> **Do this first: collect proof of your experience.** Germany's IT route and Spain's remote-work visa both accept about 3 years of documented work instead of a degree. Get:
> - experience letters on company letterhead (dates, job title, tech stack)
> - contracts, payslips or bank statements, and tax returns
> - freelance invoices and platform history
> - links to your live apps and GitHub

- UK developer jobs must pay the **£54,700** going rate for SOC 2134 ([Hunt UK Visa Sponsors](https://huntukvisasponsors.com/uk-visa-occupation-eligibility/2134-programmers-and-software-development-professionals)).

**Steps**

1. Collect proof of 3+ years' experience (see above).
2. Take IELTS and aim for 6.5 or higher to prove your English.
3. Polish your GitHub and 2–3 live projects, and write a one-page English CV.
4. Apply to 10+ jobs a week on the [visa-sponsorship boards](#b3-where-skilled-workers-should-look-for-jobs), and contact companies on the sponsor registers.
5. After an offer, the employer handles the permit. Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden take your visa application in Dhaka.

### Plan C: farm or supervisor work (backup)

Your SSC is enough and no degree is needed. Pay is low and the permit ties you to one employer, so treat this as a backup.

- **Greece:** BOESL's government seasonal farm programme takes 4,000 workers a year.
- **Italy:** seasonal farm quota. An Italian farm must file for you on the **12 January 2027** click day, so it must prepare your file before then.
- **Romania:** farm and construction jobs are on the 289-job shortage list. The employer must be authorised on WorkinRomania.gov.ro.
- **Croatia:** seasonal tourism and farm permits for up to 3 years.
- **Montenegro:** summer hotel and building jobs, and the visa is now applied for in Dhaka. Watch for a government deal; see the [Montenegro deep dive](#country-deep-dive-montenegro).

**Steps**

1. Register on [BMET OEP](https://www.oep.gov.bd/) and follow [BOESL](https://www.boesl.gov.bd) circulars.
2. Use only licensed agencies, and check the RL number before paying anything.
3. For supervisor jobs, get letters proving you supervised people.

> **Also worth a look: New Zealand.** A developer job there can lead to permanent residence after 2 years of work, with no degree and no English test for the work visa. Jobs are scarce right now, so run it next to Plan A. See [the New Zealand section](#outside-europe-new-zealand).

### Do you need the GED?

- **Not for these visas.** Work visas for developers look at your experience and the salary, not school certificates.
- **It won't open doors in Germany either.** A GED on its own does not qualify you for Germany's Opportunity Card or for direct entry to German public universities (check the [DAAD admission database](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/requirements/admission-database/)).
- **When to take it:** only if an employer asks for high-school equivalency, or if you later want to study abroad.

## OFFICIAL COSTS: doing it yourself with a job offer

*Checked 18 October 2026.*

With a real job offer you can do every step yourself and pay only government fees, the visa centre's charge, your own documents and your ticket. No agent and no "processing fee". Here is that cost for every country in this guide, in the currency each fee is charged in.

- **Tk 12,000–27,000:** what everyone pays in Bangladesh for police clearance, BMET clearance and getting documents attested ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/migrant-workers-pay-tk5500-welfare-fee-october-1550606)).
- **16 of 21** euro countries keep your own official fees abroad under €400 (table below).
- **Tk 4–15 lakh:** what Bangladeshis report paying agencies for the same visas ([AGENCY PRICES](#agency-prices-what-agencies-charge-and-what-workers-paid)).

**Who pays what:** in most countries the **employer** applies and pays for its own permit or approval. **You** pay the visa fee, the visa centre's service charge, your residence card after arrival, and your costs in Bangladesh. In Romania, Malta, Ireland and New Zealand the law also stops employers or licensed agencies from passing these costs on to you.

**Do it yourself in 7 steps**

1. **Find the job yourself** on the portals in this guide, and get a signed offer or contract.
2. **Check the employer** exists and, where there is a list, is allowed to hire foreigners.
3. **The employer applies** for your permit and pays its own fee.
4. **Book your own appointment** on the embassy or visa-centre website. Appointments are free; never buy one.
5. **Pay the visa fee yourself** at the counter or online, and keep every receipt.
6. **Get BMET clearance** on oc.bmet.gov.bd and do the pre-departure training.
7. **Buy your own ticket**, unless the employer pays for it.

## 2. How a European work visa works

1. **Find an employer.** See the job sources in [A3](#a3-where-non-skilled-workers-should-look-for-jobs), [B3](#b3-where-skilled-workers-should-look-for-jobs) and [section 7](#7-job-portals-for-foreign-workers-quick-list).
2. **Get a written job offer or contract.** Check it with the [scam checklist](#8-scams-and-safety-checklist).
3. **The employer applies for your permit in their country.** Examples:
   - Romania: the employer uses the new online platform **WorkinRomania.gov.ro**.
   - Italy: the employer applies for a **"nulla osta"** under the Decreto Flussi quota.
   - Malta: the employer applies for a **Single Permit** from Identità.
   - Germany: the employer often gets a pre-approval from the Federal Employment Agency.
4. **You apply for the long-stay "D" visa** at the embassy or visa centre.
   - 20 countries take your application in Bangladesh (Dhaka, online or by post); 13 need a trip to India. Full list: [WHERE YOU APPLY](#where-you-apply-dhaka-or-india).
   - India restarted tourist visas for Bangladeshis on **28 June 2026**, after almost two years, so the New Delhi route works again ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/nri/visa/story/india-opens-tourist-visa-window-for-bangladesh-from-june-28-after-prolonged-suspension-539229-2026-06-25)).
5. **Get BMET emigration clearance.**
   - Apply for the smart card on the government portal **[oc.bmet.gov.bd](https://oc.bmet.gov.bd)** and complete the pre-departure orientation (PDO).
   - Without clearance you can be stopped at the airport.
   - BMET no longer uses "Ami Probashi" for new clearances ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/migration/manpower-bureau-discontinues-emigration-clearance-card-training-services)).
6. **Travel, then collect your residence card** in the destination country.
7. **Learn your rights:** how to change employer, when to renew, and whether a language test is required.

## WHERE YOU APPLY: Dhaka or India

*Checked 21 October 2026.*

After your employer's permit is approved, you apply for the visa. For **20 countries** you can do that from Bangladesh: at an embassy or visa centre in Dhaka, online, or by post. For **13** you must travel to India, almost always to New Delhi.

## 3. Are you a "skilled" or "non-skilled" worker?

European immigration rules put people in two groups. Choose the section that fits you:

| | **Non-skilled / semi-skilled (Section A)** | **Skilled (Section B)** |
|---|---|---|
| Education | No university degree needed. SSC/HSC is fine. | University degree, **or** a formal vocational qualification (polytechnic diploma, 2+ year trade course, NSDA/BTEB certificate) |
| Typical jobs | Factory/production, construction helper, farm/seasonal, warehouse, cleaning, kitchen helper, hotel housekeeping, food delivery | Engineer, IT, nurse, doctor, accountant, technician, electrician/welder/mechanic **with certificates and experience**, chef with diploma |
| Main route | Employer-sponsored permit in Eastern and Southern Europe, quota systems, government-to-government programmes | Skilled-worker visas, EU Blue Card, Germany's Opportunity Card |
| Language | Usually none at the start. Croatia now requires basic Croatian (A1.1) to renew after one year. | English B2 (or IELTS) for many jobs; German A1–B1 helps a lot in Germany and Austria |
| Pay level | Near the local minimum wage | Must meet salary thresholds (often €3,000–6,000/month) |

**Tip for tradespeople:** a welder, electrician, driver, mechanic, cook or mason sits between the two groups.

- Romania, Croatia and Malta hire them as **semi-skilled workers** (Section A).
- If you have a **formal trade certificate plus 2–5 years of experience**, you may also qualify for **skilled** routes: Germany's skilled-worker visa, Austria's Red-White-Red Card for shortage jobs, Norway's skilled-worker permit (Section B).

### A2. Country notes: non-skilled / semi-skilled

#### 1) Romania (difficulty 2)

- **Quota:** 90,000 permits for non-EU workers in 2026, down from 100,000 in 2025. Employers had asked for 150,000 ([Romania Journal](https://www.romaniajournal.ro/society-people/government-to-reduce-number-of-foreign-workers-in-2026/), [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/2026-work-and-secondment-permit-quota-announced)).
- **New law:** Emergency Ordinance **GEO 32/2026**, in force since **27 April 2026** ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ro/en/insights/2026/04/access-foreign-nationals-romanian-labor-market--new-rules.html), [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/new-regulations-foreign-workers-romania-2026)).
  - All hiring of non-EU workers goes through **[WorkinRomania.gov.ro](https://workinromania.gov.ro)**. The platform went live on **6 August 2026** ([Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/platform-foreign-workers-coming-romania-and-running-aug-2026)).
  - Only jobs on the government **shortage-occupation list** are accepted, for example cook, electrician, machinery mechanic, car/van driver, welder, carpenter and mason ([list explained](https://teaha.ro/en/2026/08/07/shortage-occupations-for-foreign-workers-in-2026/)).
  - Recruitment agencies must be **authorised** and pay financial guarantees.
  - Your salary must be paid **into your own bank account**.
  - Your contract must be written in Romanian **and** in a language you understand.
- **Minimum wage:** RON 4,325 gross per month from 1 July 2026 ([Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/romania/employment-and-hr/salariul-minim-brut-a-fost-majorat-la-4325-ron-de-la-1-iulie-2026)).
- **Visa:** Romania has no embassy in Dhaka.
  - You apply to the **Romanian Embassy in New Delhi**. Appointments come through the eVisa system and are scheduled by the date of your work permit.
  - The embassy says intermediaries are not needed ([Embassy notice](https://newdelhi.mae.ro/en/local-news/1202)).
  - The new platform also covers visa requests, so check how interviews are scheduled now.
- **Schengen:** Romania has been a full Schengen member since January 2025. A Romanian residence card lets you *visit* other Schengen countries, but **not work** there.
- **Warnings:**
  - Many Bangladeshis who left their Romanian employer became undocumented ([InfoMigrants](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/44274/bangladeshi-migrants-in-romania-from-regular-to-undocumented-part-2-of-2)).
  - Private agent fees of **Tk 10–12 lakh** were reported, against **Tk 1.65 lakh** under the government arrangement ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/migration/romania-set-temporary-consular-services-dhaka-370771)).

#### 2) Croatia (difficulty 2)

- **Size of demand:**
  - Over **160,000** residence-and-work permits were issued in Jan–Nov 2025. Bangladesh got **3,183**, compared with Nepal 29,579 and India 14,500 ([Croatia Week](https://www.croatiaweek.com/foreign-workers-in-croatia-160000-permits-in-2025-where-they-come-from/)).
  - **106,395** permits were issued in Jan–Jul 2026 ([Dubrovnik Times](https://www.thedubrovniktimes.com/news/croatia/item/19723-foreign-worker-permits-in-croatia-drop-in-early-2026-tourism-sector-leads-demand)).
  - ❗ In January 2025 Croatia said it would stop issuing work permits to Bangladeshis, after 8,000 of the 12,400 people given permits and visas in 2024 never arrived ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/croatia-stop-issuing-work-permits-bangladeshi-workers-embassy-says-1054731)). Bangladeshi numbers have stayed low since, so expect strict checks.
- **Law changes in force since 4 June 2026** ([Croatia Week](https://www.croatiaweek.com/croatia-new-foreign-workers-law-2026/)):
  - ✅ You can **change employer after 6 months** without a new permit.
  - ✅ Seasonal permits can run up to 3 years.
  - ✅ You have 3 months to find a new job if you lose yours (6 months after two years).
  - ❗ You must pass a **Croatian language exam (A1.1)** to renew your permit after one year ([Croatia Week](https://www.croatiaweek.com/croatia-mandatory-croatian-language-test-foreign-workers-law/)).
  - ❗ Employers face stricter checks (financial history, domestic-worker ratios).
- **Visa:**
  - Applications from Bangladesh go through **VFS Global in Dhaka** and are decided by the Croatian Embassy in New Delhi.
  - The Honorary Consulate in Dhaka does identity verification ([VFS Croatia–Bangladesh](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/bgd/en/hrv)).
- **Job board:** [Croatian Employment Service, Burza rada](https://burzarada.hzz.hr).

#### 3) Italy (difficulty 3, but slow)

- **Quotas (Decreto Flussi 2026–2028)** ([Immigrazione Bologna](https://www.immigrazionebologna.it/en/2025/10/22/flows-decree-2026-2028/)):
  - 2026: 164,850 places (88,000 seasonal + 76,850 non-seasonal).
  - 2027: 165,850 places.
  - Bangladesh is on the list of eligible countries.
- **How it works:**
  - Only an **Italian employer** can apply for your *nulla osta* (work authorisation), on fixed "click days".
  - Click days for 2027: **12 Jan** (agriculture), **9 Feb** (tourism), **16 Feb** (general non-seasonal) and **18 Feb 2027** (home care) ([click-day dates](https://www.shimmigrations.com/decreto-flussi-2027-date-click-day/)).
  - Employers fill in applications beforehand. The 2026 pre-fill window was 23 Oct–7 Dec 2025, so find an employer **before November 2026**.
- **Extra checks for Bangladesh:**
  - Since Decree-Law 145/2024, Bangladeshi nulla osta are verified again, and the employer must confirm the job within 15 days.
  - The visa process in Dhaka starts on a VFS web form ([Italian Embassy Dhaka – work visa](https://ambdhaka.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-straniero/visti/work-visa/), [portal notice](https://ambdhaka.esteri.it/en/news/dall_ambasciata/2025/04/opening-of-work-visa-web-portal/)).
- **Warnings:**
  - Processing has sometimes taken **over a year**.
  - A June 2026 report found **1,200+ people** caught in a fake-contract visa scheme between Italy and Bangladesh. One worker paid €16,000 for a job that did not exist ([Hyphen](https://hyphenonline.com/2026/06/01/italy-visa-fraud-scheme-bangladeshis-stranded-naples-dhaka/)).
  - Never pay for a "nulla osta".

#### 4) Greece (difficulty 3)

- **Government programme:**
  - Under a 2022 agreement, Greece takes **4,000 Bangladeshi seasonal farm workers a year** for 5 years ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/nrb/migration/news/greece-signs-mou-recruit-4000-bangladeshi-workers-each-year-2958236)).
  - Workers stay up to 9 months a year, must return home, and cannot get permanent residence.
  - The government recruiter **BOESL** runs it; BOESL held a workshop on Greek jobs in August 2026 ([BSS](https://www.bssnews.net/others/414419)).
- **New law 5275/2026** (February 2026) ([EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gr/technical/tax/tax-alerts/law-5275-2026-immigration-policies)):
  - Yearly quotas by sector, which may be set per country of origin.
  - Work contracts generally at least 6 months.
  - The employer needs labour-market approval.
- **Visa:** the Greece visa centre (GVCW) in **Banani, Dhaka** accepts national D visas. Your passport must be valid for at least 1.5 years ([GVCW](https://bd-gr.gvcworld.eu/en/visa-info-long-term-national-d-visa)).
- **Official info:** [Greek Ministry of Labour – work for third-country nationals](https://ypergasias.gov.gr/en/labour-relations/individual-employment-relations/work-for-third-country-nationals-in-greece/).

#### 5) Malta (difficulty 3)

- **Permit:** the employer applies to **Identità** for a Single Permit ([Identità](https://identita.gov.mt/expatriates-unit-main-page/noneu-nationals/employment-related-permits/single-permit/)).
- **New in 2026:**
  - From **1 March 2026**, first-time applicants must pass an online **Pre-Departure Course**: about 10–12 hours, with a €250 total fee ([Identità](https://identita.gov.mt/pre-departure-course-requirement-to-be-verified-as-from-1-march-2026/), [CSB Group](https://www.csbgroup.com/articles/planning-to-employ-third-country-nationals-in-malta-new-2026-requirement-explained/)).
  - Hospitality and tourism jobs also need the **Skills Pass** ([skillspass.org.mt](https://skillspass.org.mt/)).
- **Visa:**
  - Apply at **VFS Global in Dhaka** within 60 days of the Approval-in-Principle letter.
  - VFS will not accept an application without the original permit letter ([VFS Malta–Bangladesh](https://www.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/malta-extended-services/bangladesh/english/index.html)).
- **Warnings:**
  - Wages are low; some agencies have pushed €600-a-month jobs ([The Shift](https://theshiftnews.com/2024/07/19/bangladeshi-agencies-targeting-malta-for-e600-a-month-jobs/)).
  - Bangladeshi visa processing has been frozen before ([Lovin Malta](https://lovinmalta.com/news/bangladesh-malta-visa-applications-frozen/)).
- **Jobs:** [Jobsplus vacancies](https://jobsplus.gov.mt/vacancy).
- Full details, costs and job sites: [the Malta section](#country-deep-dive-malta).

#### 6) Bulgaria (difficulty 3)

- **Permit:** single permit with a 15-day labour-market test.
- **Company caps:** an employer's foreign staff may not exceed **20%** (large companies) or **35%** (small and medium companies). A draft law may raise these caps by 5 points ([Innovires](https://www.innovires.com/en/blog/work-permit-bulgaria.html), [Labour Migration Act](https://www.mlsp.government.bg/uploads/19/zaetost/labour-migration-and-labour-mobility-act.pdf)).
- **Pay:** minimum wage about €620 a month. Bulgaria uses the euro from 2026 and is in Schengen.
- **Visa:** there is no Bulgarian embassy in Bangladesh. The **Bulgarian Embassy in New Delhi** covers Bangladesh, and since 1 November 2025 D visas are lodged at **VFS Global in New Delhi** instead of at the embassy ([Bulgarian MFA](https://www.mfa.bg/en/embassyinfo/bangladesh), [VFS Global](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/PDF/media-releases/2025/MR_Bulgaria_D_visa_services_rollout_in_India_Oct_2025.pdf)).
- **Employer attitudes:** TBS reports Bulgarian employers are becoming reluctant to hire Bangladeshis, because many leave for Western Europe ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/world/europe-job-migration-surges-46-h1-amid-gulf-slump-1475796)).

#### 7) Poland (difficulty 3)

- **Permit:** the employer gets a work permit from the voivode.
- **2026 changes:**
  - Fees rose to PLN 400.
  - There are new refusal grounds, for example very part-time jobs or employer tax debts.
  - Most procedures moved online ([Grant Thornton](https://grantthornton.pl/en/article/employment-of-foreign-nationals-in-poland-in-2026-key-changes-and-important-deadlines-in-immigration-regulations/)).
- **Visa:**
  - The **Polish Embassy in New Delhi** covers Bangladesh and takes D visas through visa centres in India, so plan a trip to New Delhi ([gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/india/d-type-national-visa)). Some guides say Dhaka, but we found no Polish visa centre there.
  - Sweden's embassy in Dhaka stopped handling *Schengen* visas for Poland after 15 April 2026 ([Swedish Embassy notice](https://www.swedenabroad.se/en/embassies/bangladesh-dhaka/current/news/notice-visa-application-poland/)).
- **Official guide:** [Work in Poland – guide for foreigners (PDF)](https://psz.praca.gov.pl/documents/10240/42353333/WORK+IN+POLAND_GUIDE+FOR+FOREIGNERS+FROM+THIRD+COUNTRIES_EN.pdf/18ebf27d-615c-2a18-2c5c-91218071464a?t=1756293815182).
- **Jobs:** [Central Job Offers Database (CBOP)](https://oferty.praca.gov.pl/portal).

#### 8) Portugal (difficulty 3.5)

- **Job-seeker visa gone:** the old visa that let you come and search for work was abolished by **Law 61/2025 on 23 October 2025**.
  - Its replacement is only for **highly qualified** people, and was still reported as not operational in mid-2026 ([Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/portugal-significant-immigration-reforms-in-effect.html), [Portugal visa portal](https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/highlights/change-in-the-regime-regarding-work-seeking-visa-applications), [Grow in Portugal](https://www.growinportugal.com/news/portugal-s-job-seeker-visa-now-locked-to-highly-skilled-prof-2026-07-18)).
- **Work visa (D1):** you need a real job contract.
  - Bangladeshi applications are handled by the **Embassy of Portugal in New Delhi**.
  - Bangladeshi police certificates must first be legalised ([Embassy of Portugal, New Delhi](https://novadeli.embaixadaportugal.mne.gov.pt/en/)).

#### 9) Slovakia (difficulty 3.5)

- **Demand:** shortages in driving, warehouses, construction and healthcare.
- **July 2026 change:** since **15 July 2026**, you must stay in the job your permit was issued for ([Envoy](https://www.envoyglobal.com/news-alert/slovakia-implements-foreign-worker-employment-changes/), [IOM Migration Info Centre](https://mic.iom.sk/en/work/work-permit.html)).
- **Visa:** apply at the **Slovak Embassy in New Delhi**, booking by e-mail ([Slovak MFA New Delhi](https://www.mzv.sk/en/web/dilli-en/visa-and-services/schengen-visa)).

#### 10) Cyprus (difficulty 3.5)

- **Permitted sectors:** agriculture, livestock, manufacturing, trade and food delivery.
- **Rules:** the employer needs Department of Labour approval, and there are company caps on foreign staff.
- **Existing workforce:** Bangladeshis are among the main nationalities in the 26,710 general-employment permits (August 2026) ([Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/09/27/cyprus-labour-shortages-who-can-legally-work), [employer guide](https://gk-lawfirm.com/practice-areas/immigration-law/cyprus-work-permit/)).
- **Visa:** apply at VFS Global in Dhaka; the Cyprus High Commission in New Delhi decides ([Visa Bangla](https://visabangla.com/travel/cyprus.html)).
- **Not the north:** only the Republic of Cyprus (the Greek-Cypriot south) is in the EU. Visas for Northern Cyprus, sold as a way into Europe, are a trap. Details, costs and job sites: [the Cyprus section](#country-deep-dive-cyprus).

#### 11) Harder options: Slovenia, Lithuania, Latvia, Bosnia, Montenegro (difficulty 4)

- **Lithuania:** the 2026 quota is **24,706**, down from 40,250 in 2024, and the industry quota ran out early ([Bimaris](https://bimaris.legal/insights/lithuanian-work-permit), [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2415719/lithuanian-industry-exhausts-quota-for-hiring-third-country-nationals)).
- **Latvia:** in August 2026 the Interior Ministry proposed quotas and limits on visas for countries without readmission agreements ([Inbox.eu](https://news.inbox.eu/150kwwc-latvia-may-sharply-limit-entry-for-foreigners-ministry-of-the-interior-proposes-introducing-quotas?language=en)).
- **Slovenia:** single permit through the Employment Service ([ZRSZ](https://www.ess.gov.si/en/jobseekers/employment-of-non-eu-migrant-workers/work-in-slovenia/single-permit)). Employers there are reported to be reluctant to hire Bangladeshis.
- **Where you apply:** Slovenia, Latvia and Bosnia take visas at their embassies in New Delhi, and Lithuania at VFS centres in India, so all four need an India trip ([WHERE YOU APPLY](#where-you-apply-dhaka-or-india)).
- **Bosnia and Montenegro:**
  - Permit quotas for 2026: Bosnia **7,427**, Montenegro **28,988**.
  - Wages are low.
  - These countries are often misused as transit routes ([Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=a122552e-d60a-4756-b99f-cbe7acbddb2f), [Rona Legal](https://www.ronalegal.com/en/blog/working-in-montenegro-from-bangladesh)).
  - **Montenegro update (October 2026):** a visiting MP said Montenegro needs 50,000+ workers a year and is open to a deal with Bangladesh, but nothing is signed. Full details in the [Montenegro deep dive](#country-deep-dive-montenegro).

#### ⚠️ Serbia and North Macedonia (on hold)

- **Serbia's process is simple on paper:**
  - One online "single permit" for residence and work, valid up to 3 years ([Welcome to Serbia](https://welcometoserbia.gov.rs/residence-and-work-permit)).
  - A D visa from the Serbian Embassy (New Delhi covers Bangladesh) ([Serbian MFA](https://www.mfa.gov.rs/en/citizens/travel-serbia/visa-regime/banglades)).
- **But BMET clearance is held up:** on **24 September 2026**, visa holders protested in Dhaka because BMET's manpower clearance for **Serbia, North Macedonia and Moldova** is held up ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/visas-holders-urge-bmet-resume-manpower-clearance-serbia-north-macedonia-moldova-1552981)).
- **Forced-labour allegations:** in January 2026, rights groups alleged forced labour of Bangladeshi workers at a Serbian tyre factory ([BHRRC](https://www.business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/serbia-rights-groups-allege-human-trafficking-and-forced-labor-involving-bangladeshi-workers-at-linglong-tire-plant-company-did-not-comment/)).
- **Not an EU route:** Serbia is not in the EU, and a Serbian permit does **not** let you work in the EU.

### A3. Where non-skilled workers should look for jobs

For factory, farm, construction and hotel jobs, most real offers come through **licensed recruiting agencies** or **government programmes**, not through job websites. Use these, in this order:

1. **Bangladesh government channels (safest)**
   - **[BMET Overseas Employment Platform (OEP)](https://www.oep.gov.bd/)**: register as an aspirant migrant and see verified overseas employers and jobs. Check whether an agency is licensed on the **[OEP agency list](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies)**.
   - **[BOESL](https://www.boesl.gov.bd)**, the government-owned recruiter. It runs government-to-government schemes such as Greece seasonal farm work, and publishes circulars. Recruitment portal: [brms.boesl.gov.bd](https://brms.boesl.gov.bd/).
   - **[BAIRA member list](https://baira.org.bd)**: a second check on a private agency's licence.
   - **EU–Bangladesh Talent Partnership (ILO)**: EU-funded training in 8 trades. About 700 workers had finished by 2026, mainly for jobs in **Italy**. Watch BMET/BOESL notices ([ILO](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/talent-partnership-triple-win-bangladesh-eu-member-states-and-migrant), [Bangladesh Monitor](https://www.bangladeshmonitor.com.bd/en/698-bangladeshis-ready-for-jobs-in-europe-under-eu-talent-partnership)).
2. **Official job portals of the destination countries** (you can check that the employer and job are real)
   - Romania: **[WorkinRomania.gov.ro](https://workinromania.gov.ro)**. It is the only legal channel now, so your employer must be registered and authorised there.
   - Croatia: **[HZZ Burza rada](https://burzarada.hzz.hr)**
   - Malta: **[Jobsplus vacancies](https://jobsplus.gov.mt/vacancy)**
   - Poland: **[CBOP job database](https://oferty.praca.gov.pl/portal)**
   - Serbia: **[National Employment Service (NSZ)](https://www.nsz.gov.rs)**
   - Montenegro: **[Employment Agency of Montenegro](https://www.zzzcg.me/)** · [Zaposli.me](https://zaposli.me/oglasi-za-posao/crna-gora)
   - Cyprus (the Republic of Cyprus only): **[Ergodotisi](https://www.ergodotisi.com/)** · [CyprusWork](https://www.cypruswork.com/)
   - Mauritius (outside Europe): **[Mauritius Jobs](https://mauritiusjobs.govmu.org/)** · [MyJob.mu](https://www.myjob.mu/)
   - All EU: **[EURES](https://eures.europa.eu)**
3. **Italy specifically:** there is no public job board for Decreto Flussi. You need an Italian employer willing to file on the click day. Use BOESL/BMET programmes and the Talent Partnership, and **never pay for a nulla osta**.

### B2. Country notes: skilled

#### 1) Germany: the best option for skilled Bangladeshis (difficulty 3)

- **Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte).** This is a job-search residence permit for up to 1 year.
  - While searching you may work part-time and do trial work.
  - It needs:
    - a university degree **or** a 2-year+ vocational qualification recognised in Bangladesh;
    - **German A1 or English B2**;
    - proof of funds of about **€1,091/month** (€13,092 a year, e.g. in a blocked account);
    - **either** full recognition in Germany **or** at least **6 points**. Points come from qualification, experience, language, age (≤35 = 2 points) and ties to Germany.
  - Sources: [Expatrio](https://www.expatrio.com/opportunity-card), [How to Germany](https://www.how-to-germany.com/visa/long-stay/opportunity-card/).
  - Apply at the **German Embassy in Dhaka** ([Embassy info](https://dhaka.diplo.de/bd-en/service/2685670-2685670)).
- **Skilled-worker visa:** job offer plus a qualification recognised in Germany ([Embassy info](https://dhaka.diplo.de/bd-en/service/2679664-2679664)).
- **EU Blue Card 2026:** €50,700/year, or **€45,934.20** for shortage jobs (IT, engineering, science, health), recent graduates and IT specialists without a degree ([RT Partner](https://www.rtpartner.de/en/immigration/blaue-karte-eu-mindestgehalt-2026/)).
- **Vocational training (Ausbildung):** a paid apprenticeship, for example in nursing, trades or hospitality. It usually needs German at B1 level.
- **Processing:**
  - The embassy takes online applications through the Consular Services Portal.
  - Processing is about 3–6 weeks for work visas and about 8 weeks for the Opportunity Card, **plus appointment waiting time** ([Embassy FAQ](https://dhaka.diplo.de/bd-en/service/2689548-2689548), [National visa page](https://dhaka.diplo.de/bd-en/service/2682868-2682868)).
- **Before you apply:** check your university in the **anabin** database (status "H+") and start recognition early: [anabin.kmk.org](https://anabin.kmk.org), [anerkennung-in-deutschland.de](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de).

#### 2) Finland (difficulty 3)

- **Salary:** the general minimum income for an employee permit is **€1,600/month** (2026) or the collective-agreement wage. Specialist and EU Blue Card permits need **€3,937/month** ([Migri](https://migri.fi/en/working-in-finland/income-requirement)).
- **How to apply:**
  - Apply online through Enter Finland, then prove your identity at Finland's visa centre in **New Delhi**.
  - Bangladeshi documents may need extra verification ([Finland abroad – Bangladesh](https://finlandabroad.fi/web/bgd/residence-permits-to-finland)).

#### 3) Cyprus (difficulty 3)

- **Fast-track at foreign-owned companies:** tech, shipping and pharma companies registered with the Business Facilitation Unit can hire non-EU staff at **€2,500 a month before tax**, with a degree **or 2 years' relevant experience** and a contract of at least 2 years. No labour-market test ([Zeno Legal](https://www.zenolegal.com/resources/cyprus-work-permit-non-eu-employer-sponsorship-2026)).
- **EU Blue Card** (since July 2025): **€43,632 a year**; IT staff can show 3 years' experience in the last 7 instead of a degree ([Rightax](https://rightax.com.cy/cyprus-eu-blue-card-guide/)).
- **Visa:** apply at VFS Global in Dhaka; the High Commission in New Delhi decides ([Visa Bangla](https://visabangla.com/travel/cyprus.html)).
- Full details, costs and job sites: [the Cyprus section](#country-deep-dive-cyprus).

#### 4) Austria (difficulty 3.5)

- **Red-White-Red Card for shortage occupations:**
  - Covers 64 nationwide and 66 regional occupations in 2026.
  - Needs **55 of 90 points** and collective-agreement pay, with no labour-market test ([migration.gv.at](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/skilled-workers-in-shortage-occupations/)).
- **"Other key workers":** €3,465/month plus a labour-market test.
- **Where to apply:** Bangladeshi applications are handled by the **Austrian Embassy in New Delhi** ([BMEIA](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/entry-and-residence/settlement-and-residence)).

#### 5) Ireland (difficulty 3.5)

- **Salary floors from 1 March 2026** ([MRCI](https://www.mrci.ie/2026/03/06/new-employment-permit-salary-thresholds-from-1-march-2026/)):
  - General Employment Permit: **€36,605**
  - Critical Skills: **€40,904** with a relevant degree, or €68,911 without
  - Healthcare assistants, meat and horticulture workers: **€32,691**
- **Where to apply:** apply online, then post your papers to the **Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi** (or use an agent at VFS Kolkata). Bangladeshis give no biometrics, so no trip is needed ([Ireland.ie](https://www.ireland.ie/en/india/newdelhi/services/visas/visas-for-ireland/)).

#### 6) Spain (difficulty 4)

- **Work authorisation:** the employer applies. There is **no labour-market test for jobs on the "hard-to-fill occupations" catalogue**.
- **Other routes:**
  - Collective hiring in origin (GECCO 2026) ([ARC Legal](https://www.arc-legal.es/en/gecco-2026-spain-regulates-collective-hiring-of-foreign-workers-at-origin/)).
  - A 12-month job-seeking visa for catalogue occupations ([Giambrone](https://www.giambronelaw.com/site/news-articles-press/library/articles/new-job-seeker-visa-in-spain)).
- **Where to apply:** the **Spanish Embassy in Dhaka / BLS** ([BLS Spain Bangladesh](https://bgd.blsspainvisa.com/long-term-visa.php)).

#### 7) France (difficulty 4)

- **"Talent" permits:** reference salary €39,582. The EU Blue Card needs €59,373, or €47,498 for STEM shortage roles.
- **Shortage jobs:** jobs on the *métiers en tension* list are exempt from the labour-market test until December 2026 ([Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/france-changes-to-talent-permit-scheme-processing-timeframes-and-minimum-salary-levels.html)).
- **Language:** French is a big advantage.

#### 8) Netherlands (difficulty 4)

- **Highly skilled migrant salary thresholds, 2026:**
  - **€5,942/month** at age 30+
  - **€4,357** under 30
  - **€3,122** for recent graduates
  - Holiday pay is not counted ([Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/netherlands-highly-skilled-migrant-salary-thresholds-2026)).
- **Sponsor:** the employer must be an IND **recognised sponsor**. You can search the [public register](https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors/public-register-work).

#### 9) Sweden (difficulty 4)

- **Salary:** from 1 June 2026 it must be at least **90% of the median wage**, which was **SEK 34,470/month** as of 16 June 2026 ([Migrationsverket](https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/employers/news-archive-for-employers/news/2026-06-16-new-median-salary-affects-the-salary-requirement-for-work-permits.html)).

#### 10) Denmark (difficulty 4.5)

- **Salary schemes** ([Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/denmark/udenlandsk-arbejdskraft---nye-bel%C3%B8bsgr%C3%A6nser-under-bel%C3%B8bsordningerne-og-opdateret-positivlister)):
  - Pay Limit Scheme: **DKK 552,000/yr**
  - Supplementary scheme: **DKK 446,000/yr** (the job must be advertised on Jobnet and EURES first)
  - Or a job on the **Positive Lists**
- **Where to apply:** biometrics at **VFS Dhaka** ([Danish Embassy Dhaka](https://bangladesh.um.dk/en/travel-and-residence/practical-information/residence-permit/how-and-where-to-apply)).

#### 11) Norway (difficulty 4.5)

- **Qualification:** the skilled-worker permit needs a degree or a trade certificate.
- **Salary from 1 May 2026:** **NOK 545,400** for jobs requiring a bachelor's degree, **NOK 624,700** for jobs requiring a master's ([UDI](https://www.udi.no/en/want-to-apply/work-immigration/skilled-workers/), [Relocation.no](https://relocation.no/news-from-the-norwegian-directorate-of-immigration-udi-skilled-worker-salary-requirements-in-norway-increased-from-1-may-2026/)).
- **Where to apply:** VFS in Dhaka, with the Danish Embassy representing Norway ([Norway in Bangladesh](https://www.norway.no/en/bangladesh/services-info/visitors-visa-res-permit/res-permit/)).

#### 12) United Kingdom (difficulty 4.5)

- **Skilled Worker visa:**
  - Degree-level jobs only (RQF 6, since 22 July 2025)
  - Pay of **£41,700** or the going rate
  - **English B2** from 8 January 2026 ([Centuro Global](https://www.centuroglobal.com/articles/uk-immigration-2026-changes/), [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa))
- **Care workers:** overseas recruitment of care workers has been closed since July 2025.
- **Sponsors:** only employers on the [register of licensed sponsors](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers) can hire you.

#### 13) Estonia and Switzerland (hardest)

- **Estonia:** national immigration quota of **1,292** for 2026. Short-term employment must pay at least the average salary (€2,092 in 2025) ([Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/estonia-digital-nomad-visa-income-requirement-immigration-quota), [Estonian Interior Ministry](https://www.siseministeerium.ee/en/activities/efficient-population-management/migration)).
- **Estonia's visa:** apply for the D visa at the **Estonian Embassy in New Delhi**; the embassies in Dhaka that issue some Estonian short-stay visas cannot take D visas ([Estonian Embassy New Delhi](https://newdelhi.mfa.ee/for-bangladesh-nepal-sri-lanka-citizens/)).
- **Switzerland:** only **8,500** permits for all non-EU nationals in 2026 (4,500 B and 4,000 L), and only for highly qualified people ([Swiss Federal Council](https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/7HwBjdg5HpBA)).

## 8. Scams and safety checklist

1. **Never pay for a "job offer", "work permit" or "nulla osta".**
   - Real European employers pay their own recruitment costs.
   - Under fair-recruitment standards, workers should not pay recruitment fees.
2. **Only use BMET-licensed agencies.** Check the RL number and status ("Active") on the [OEP agency list](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies), then check again on [BAIRA](https://baira.org.bd).
3. **Check that the employer exists:** company website, business register, Google Maps, reviews.
   - In Romania, the employer must be **authorised on WorkinRomania.gov.ro**.
   - In Italy, the nulla osta must be confirmed by the immigration office (SUI).
   - In Malta, VFS only accepts an original Identità approval letter.
4. **Get the contract in a language you understand.** Romania now requires this. Check the salary, hours, housing costs and who pays the flight.
5. **Nobody may keep your passport.** Employers holding passports is a sign of forced labour.
6. **Don't travel on a visit visa to "convert" it** into a work permit, and don't use Serbia or Bosnia as a "stepping stone" to the EU.
7. **Get BMET clearance before you travel.** Keep copies of everything, and register with the Bangladesh embassy in your destination.
8. **Know the real costs.** National visa fees are about €75–120. Tk 10–15 lakh agent packages are a sign of exploitation (see the Romania and Italy notes above).

## COUNTRY DEEP DIVE: Cyprus

*Checked 20 October 2026. Cyprus uses the euro. Amounts are in the currency you pay them in: taka in Bangladesh, US dollars for the flight, euros in Cyprus.*

**Short answer:** the Republic of Cyprus is an EU country short of workers on farms, in factories, shops, delivery and building, and its foreign-owned tech companies can hire developers **without a degree**. The employer applies for you. Avoid anyone who offers "Cyprus" through the Turkish-Cypriot north.

> **Greek Cyprus or Turkish Cyprus?** The Cyprus in this guide is the **Republic of Cyprus**: the Greek-Cypriot south, an EU member. **Northern Cyprus** is run by Turkish Cypriots, is recognised only by Turkey and is not in the EU. Agents sell its student visas as a back door to Europe. Cyprus says about 9 in 10 irregular migrants arrive through Turkey and the north and then cross the UN buffer zone ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/how-bangladeshi-migrants-end-cyprus-450078)). Most Bangladeshis who do this are refused asylum.

**Key numbers**

- **26,710** general-employment work permits in force in August 2026; Bangladesh is one of the main nationalities ([Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/09/27/cyprus-labour-shortages-who-can-legally-work)).
- **€1,088** minimum wage a month before tax in 2026–2027; €979 in your first 6 months with an employer ([Sigmalive](https://en.sigmalive.com/cyprus-minimum-wage-set-at-e1088-from-january-1-2026)).
- **€2,500** a month before tax gets a developer a fast permit at a foreign-owned company, with a degree or 2 years' experience ([Zeno Legal](https://www.zenolegal.com/resources/cyprus-work-permit-non-eu-employer-sponsorship-2026)).
- **3%** of Bangladeshis' asylum claims in the EU succeeded in 2025: crossing from the north to claim asylum is no way in ([EUAA](https://www.euaa.europa.eu/latest-asylum-trends-annual-analysis/applications)).

### Route 1: developer job at a foreign-owned company (best fit)

- **Why it fits you:** foreign-owned companies, many of them in tech around Limassol, can hire non-EU staff on a fast-track that accepts **2 years of relevant experience instead of a degree**. There is no labour-market test and no cap on these staff ([Zeno Legal](https://www.zenolegal.com/resources/cyprus-work-permit-non-eu-employer-sponsorship-2026)).
- **You need:**
  - a job paying at least **€2,500 a month before tax**, on a contract of at least 2 years;
  - a degree, or proof of 2 years' relevant experience: letters, contracts and your portfolio;
  - an employer registered with the Business Facilitation Unit ([Business in Cyprus](https://www.businessincyprus.gov.cy/)).
- **Good to know:**
  - Permits take about a month and last up to 3 years, renewable.
  - The **EU Blue Card** (€43,632 a year) also accepts 3 years' IT experience instead of a degree ([Rightax](https://rightax.com.cy/cyprus-eu-blue-card-guide/)).

### Route 2: farm, factory, shop, delivery or building job (employer route)

- **Jobs open to you:** agriculture, livestock, food delivery, manufacturing, wholesale and retail, construction and other short-staffed jobs ([Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/09/27/cyprus-labour-shortages-who-can-legally-work)).
- **How it works:**
  - The employer first offers the job to local and EU workers through the public employment service.
  - The Department of Labour approves the vacancy (about 3 months), then the Migration Department issues your entry permit.
  - Each company may hire only a limited share of non-EU staff.
- **Pay and limits:** at least €979 a month for your first 6 months with an employer, then €1,088 ([Sigmalive](https://en.sigmalive.com/cyprus-minimum-wage-set-at-e1088-from-january-1-2026)). A general-employment permit lasts at most 4 years in total (6 in farming and livestock) ([AGP Law](https://www.agplaw.com/?p=634)).

### Route 3: digital nomad visa (Plan B)

- Live in Cyprus while you work for employers or clients outside Cyprus: 1 year at first, up to 3 years in total.
- You need at least **€3,500 a month after tax**, health insurance and a clean police record. There are 1,000 places a year since 30 October 2025 ([Zeno Legal](https://www.zenolegal.com/resources/cyprus-digital-nomad-visa-2026)).

### From job offer to residence permit, step by step

1. **Find an employer:** Ergodotisi, Carierista or CyprusWork; for the fast-track, foreign-owned tech companies.

7. **Card in Malta:** do the health screening and give your biometrics, then collect your residence card.

## COUNTRY DEEP DIVE: Montenegro

*Checked 18 October 2026. Montenegro uses the euro. Amounts are in the currency you pay them in: taka in Bangladesh, US dollars for the flight, euros in Montenegro.*

**Short answer:** Montenegro is short of summer workers and has started talking to Bangladesh about hiring through a government deal, but **nothing is signed**. Today, jobs go through a yearly permit quota that Turkish, Serbian and Russian workers mostly fill. The good news: Bangladeshis can now apply for the visa **in Dhaka**, at VFS Global.

> **What you probably saw.** On 5 October 2026, Montenegrin MP Gordan Stojović told Bangladesh's State Minister for Foreign Affairs in Dhaka that his country needs more than 50,000 workers a year, in tourism, construction, hospitality and caregiving, and is open to hiring Bangladeshis through government-to-government agreements ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/foreign-policy/dhaka-podgorica-discuss-trade-investment-labour-cooperation-1563691), [Bangladesh Pratidin](https://en.bd-pratidin.com/national/2026/10/05/71337)). ## OUTSIDE EUROPE: Mauritius

**Short answer:** Mauritius, an island country in the Indian Ocean where business runs in English and French, has hired Bangladeshis for decades, mostly for garment factories. 2. **The employer applies:** an Occupation Permit online at the EDB (MUR 50,000+), or a work permit from the labour ministry for other jobs.
3. **Approval, then travel:** travel only after the permit is approved; for a work permit you have 6 months to arrive ([labour ministry guidelines](https://empment-labour.govmu.org/Documents/Guidelines%20for%20Work%20Permit%20Application%20June%202016_V1.pdf)).
4. **Papers and BMET clearance:** police clearance, attested documents and BMET clearance (Tk 6,500).
5. **Fly to Mauritius:** about US$423–835 one way with one stop.
6. **Residence permit** at the Passport and Immigration Office; the employer pays MUR 500 a year.

### Know before you go

- **One employer only.** Your work permit is for one employer. Workers who leave become irregular: in March 2026 Mauritius counted 6,926 foreigners overstaying, 2,819 of them reported missing by their employer ([L'Express via AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202604010677.html)).

- **Fees are still charged.** A Bangladeshi garment worker borrowed US$2,300 to pay his agent. Brands later repaid nearly US$390,000 in fees to 637 Bangladeshi workers at his factory ([Transparentem](https://transparentem.org/report/mauritius-disclosure/)).
- **No work on a tourist visa.** Someone who enters Mauritius as a tourist may not work. Travel only after your permit is approved ([Australian High Commission](https://mauritius.embassy.gov.au/plut/Working_in_Mauritius.html)).
- **Your embassy is in Port Louis.** The Bangladesh High Commission's labour wing: fsportlouis@probashi.gov.bd, +230 212 9527 ([Expatriates' Welfare Ministry](https://wewb.portal.gov.bd/site/page/847ba196-b014-49b4-b1f9-de178dba156e)).

## OUTSIDE EUROPE: New Zealand

*Checked 8 October 2026. Amounts are in the currency you pay them in: NZ dollars (NZ$) in New Zealand, taka in Bangladesh, US dollars for the flight. Pay is NZ$ an hour before tax; "a year" means 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.*

**Short answer:** yes, New Zealand takes skilled workers from abroad, but only through employers that Immigration New Zealand (INZ) has accredited, and there is no special quota for Bangladeshis. For you, the realistic route is a **developer job on an Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV)**, then **permanent residence after 2 years** through the Skilled Migrant Category. No degree needed.

> **What you probably saw.** Bangladeshi news sites reported New Zealand's changes as "two new visas" and "new rules for skilled workers". The two new visas are seasonal farm and tourism visas, and both ask for earlier seasonal experience. The bigger news for you is the residence pathway that opened on 24 August 2026: it counts work experience instead of a degree.

### Route 1: developer job on a work visa (AEWV), best fit

- **Why it fits you:** no degree needed. Two years of provable developer work passes the skills test, and developer jobs (skill level 1) need no English test for this visa.
- **You need:**
  - a job offer from an **accredited employer**: 30+ hours a week, paid the market rate and at least the NZ$23.95 minimum wage;
  - **2+ years** of relevant, verifiable experience (or a Level 4+ qualification, or pay of NZ$70+ an hour);
  - a medical at IOM Dhaka and a police certificate.
- **Good to know:**
  - The visa lasts up to 5 years for skill level 1–3 jobs ([INZ](https://immigration.govt.nz/work/requirements-for-work-visas/how-long-you-can-work-on-work-visas/how-long-you-can-stay-on-an-aewv)). It is tied to your employer; a new employer needs its own job check.
  - Your partner can get an open work visa if you earn NZ$28.00+ an hour in a skill level 1–3 job ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/process-to-apply/once-you-have-a-visa/bringing-family-to-new-zealand/bringing-family-if-you-have-a-work-visa/bringing-family-if-you-have-an-accredited-employer-work-visa-aewv/)).
  - Fee NZ$1,540. About 4 in 5 decisions take 7 weeks or less ([Visa Atlas](https://visaatlas.org/processing-times/new-zealand/accredited-employer-work-visa)).

### Route 2: residence after 2 years (Skilled Migrant Category), the goal

Since 24 August 2026, experience and New Zealand pay can stand in for a degree ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/resident-visas-to-live-in-new-zealand/skilled-residence-pathways-in-new-zealand/skilled-migrant-category-pathway-to-residence/2026-changes-to-the-skilled-migrant-category-resident-visa/)).

- **Skilled Work Experience pathway:** 5 years' relevant experience in a skill level 1–3 job, including 2 years in New Zealand at NZ$38.50+ an hour (1.1 × median). Amber-list jobs (such as ICT support and web administrator) need all 5 years in New Zealand at NZ$42.00 (1.2 × median). Red-list jobs can't use it. Developer jobs are skill level 1 and were not on the red or amber lists in our sources, but check INZ's lists for your exact job title.
- **6-point route:** you need 6 points, from pay plus New Zealand work. New Zealand work gives 1 point after 12 months, 2 after 18 months and 3 after 2 years; it only counts if it is full-time, skilled and paid at least NZ$35.00 an hour.

| Pay (NZ$ an hour) | Points from pay | New Zealand work needed for the rest |
|---|---|---|
| 52.50 (1.5 × median) | 3 | 2 years (3 points) |
| 70.00 (2 × median) | 4 | 18 months (2 points) |
| 105.00 (3 × median) | 6 | none |

- **Also needed:** age 55 or under, IELTS 6.5 or equal, health and character checks, and a job paying at least NZ$35.00 an hour. Fee NZ$6,450; the Expression of Interest is free ([Visa Atlas](https://visaatlas.org/fees/new-zealand/skilled-migrant-category)).
- **Shortcut, probably not for you:** Software Engineer is on the Green List's straight-to-residence tier, but it asks for a relevant degree or a long experience record (one guide says 7+ years) and a set salary. Check [INZ's Green List](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/resident-visas-to-live-in-new-zealand/skilled-residence-pathways-in-new-zealand/green-list-pathway-to-residence) for your exact job.

**What developers earn:** Payscale's averages are about NZ$73,200 a year early in a career (NZ$35.19 an hour) and NZ$89,200 mid-career (NZ$42.88 an hour) ([Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/research/NZ/Job=Software_Developer/Salary/fb624084/Mid-Career)). So the NZ$38.50 bar for the new pathway is within reach, and NZ$52.50 is a stretch.

### Route 3: farm and seasonal work, backup

- **Global Workforce Seasonal Visa:** 3 seasons in a similar job in the last 6 years. Up to 9 months' work a year for 3 years. No English test; the employer invites you ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/global-workforce-seasonal-visa)).
- **Peak Seasonal Visa:** 1 season in the last 3 years. Up to 7 months, then 4 months outside New Zealand. Private health insurance for jobs longer than 3 months ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/peak-seasonal-visa)).
- **Dairy farm assistant on an AEWV:** a skill level 4–5 job, so you need 2 years' farm experience and IELTS 4.0 or equal. Ads pay about NZ$26–30 an hour. The stay is capped at 3 years, and your family can't get visas through this job ([Farm Source Jobs](https://jobs.nzfarmsource.co.nz/)).
- The seasonal rules don't say your seasons must be in New Zealand, so a farm season in Greece or Italy (Plan C) may count. Confirm with INZ first. Partners and children can't join on either seasonal visa. The Pacific-only RSE scheme and the working holiday visa are not open to Bangladeshis.

### From Dhaka to residence, step by step

1. **Prove 2+ years of developer work:** experience letters, contracts, payslips and tax records that INZ can verify.
2. **Find an accredited employer** on Seek, LinkedIn or Trade Me Jobs, and check it on [INZ's list](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/new-zealand-visas/preparing-a-visa-application/working-in-nz/check-if-an-employer-is-accredited).
3. **The employer passes a job check** (it pays NZ$735) and sends you a **job token**, your link to apply.
4. **Get your medical and police certificate:** medical at IOM Dhaka (Tk 10,100) and a Bangladesh police clearance.
5. **Apply online for the AEWV:** NZ$1,540.
6. **BMET clearance, then fly:** Tk 6,500 for the welfare fee and insurance; Dhaka → Auckland about US$640.
7. **After 2 years, apply for residence** through the Skilled Migrant Category, with IELTS 6.5 or equal.

### Job market, scams and your rights

- **Jobs are scarce right now.** Unemployment was 5.6% in the June 2026 quarter, the highest in more than a decade ([Stats NZ via Mirage News](https://www.miragenews.com/unemployment-rate-at-5-6-percent-in-june-2026-1721808/)). Most employers hire people already in the country, and many ads say "must have NZ work rights". Apply widely and say early that you need an accredited employer.
- **When it goes wrong:**
  - **Auckland, 2023, NZ$15,000–40,000:** 115 Bangladeshi and Indian workers arrived on work visas to find no jobs after paying agents this much. They were living in crowded, unsanitary houses; the count later reached 144 ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/686434)).
  - **Auckland, 2026, NZ$44,750:** a migrant paid this for a landscaping job. The company was fined NZ$16,000 and must repay him, and its director must pay him NZ$8,000 more ([Employment New Zealand](https://www.employment.govt.nz/news-and-updates/auckland-company-charged-migrant-worker-almost-45000-for-his-job)).
  - **Kuala Lumpur, 2024:** 25 Bangladeshi men were stopped from boarding a flight to Auckland. A middleman had apparently convinced them they held valid work visas ([1News](https://www.1news.co.nz/2024/05/08/25-men-caught-trying-to-enter-nz-without-proper-visas/)).
- **Rules that protect you:**
  - Employers may not take money (a "premium") for giving you a job or letting you keep it. The Labour Inspectorate makes them pay it back, with penalties ([Employment NZ](https://www.employment.govt.nz/news-and-updates/auckland-company-charged-migrant-worker-almost-45000-for-his-job)).
  - An accredited employer must pay all recruitment costs, in New Zealand and overseas, and cannot pass them on to you ([INZ](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/work/worker-rights/what-your-employer-must-do-when-they-hire-you-on-an-aewv)).
  - Anyone giving New Zealand visa advice, in Dhaka too, needs a licence from the Immigration Advisers Authority unless they are a New Zealand lawyer. Check the free register ([IAA](https://iaa.govt.nz/for-migrants)).
  - Scammers pose as INZ staff or licensed advisers. INZ officers do not call you asking for payment ([INZ via Scoop](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/AK2601/S00291.htm)).
- **Before you pay anyone, check that:**
  - the employer is accredited: it is on INZ's list, or it showed you its accreditation and job check;
  - the job token came from the employer, and you applied on INZ's own website and paid INZ directly;
  - you paid nobody for the job offer;
  - your adviser is on the IAA register, or is a New Zealand lawyer;
  - your contract shows the job title, the pay and at least 30 hours a week;
  - you will get BMET clearance before you fly.
